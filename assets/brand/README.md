# TESSERRA logo

The primary TESSERRA mark is the one-color `tesserra-logo.svg` master. Its three linked right-angle forms represent authority passing through an attenuated delegation chain, ending in a recorded provenance point.

- The SVG inherits `currentColor`.
- Use copper (`#b97955`) in the dark product shell and the foreground color in monochrome contexts.
- Recommended sizes: 24–25 px in navigation, 32 px for app icons, and 16 or 32 px for favicons. Do not render below 16 px.
- Preserve clear space equal to at least 25% of the rendered width.
- Do not rotate, outline, put the mark in a decorative container, recolor individual segments, or alter its geometry.
- Beside the visible TESSERRA wordmark, render it as decorative (`aria-hidden="true"`) and label the containing home control.
