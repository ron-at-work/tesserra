# Milestone 1 requirements traceability ledger

**Ledger status:** draft. This is the controlling governance index for the approved ATTEST reference-implementation plan. It maps every plan section to evidence that must exist before implementation is unlocked. A present artifact is review input, not approval evidence; the [Milestone 1 review checklist](milestone-1-review-checklist.md) remains authoritative.

**Status values:** Draft, Verified, Blocked, Approved. Only a complete, approved Milestone 1 gate recorded in the [checklist](milestone-1-review-checklist.md#approval-record) unlocks implementation.

**Anchor notation:** `RFC §n` references [RFC 0001](rfcs/0001-attest-v1-wire-protocol.md); `CTRL-nn` references the [threat-model control catalogue](security/threat-model.md#6-control-catalogue). Each row has the exact applicable RFC section and threat control(s). A governance-only mapping uses the closest protocol security control that its review protects.

## Trace completion rules

A row may move to **Verified** only when its exact RFC section, threat control ID, linked architecture/standards record, and stated evidence can be located. A critical/high threat and every security-critical wire choice must additionally have a named fixture or a documented, accepted untestable assumption. Do not use a future-phase promise as evidence that a Milestone 1 protocol decision is complete.

When an approved artifact path or anchor changes, update this ledger and the [checklist](milestone-1-review-checklist.md) in the same review change. Do not mark an independent-reproduction or approval item complete until its reviewer evidence exists.
